Scope & intent

This project is an analytical and educational exercise in existential-AI-safety reasoning. It synthesizes published academic work and public reporting to evaluate a hand-drawn threat model — it does not endorse, condone, or describe any specific operational system. All severity scores are illustrative modeling outputs, not measured probabilities. See About & Methodology for sources and limitations.
