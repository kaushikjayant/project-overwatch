The deepest driver of autonomy is not a single bad actor but a strategic dilemma. If one nation
automates lethal decisions, every rival must follow — or fight at a permanent time disadvantage.

## The Nash trap

Automating is a dominant strategy for each side — which means the stable outcome is the one neither
wants: a runaway race where reaction windows collapse to microseconds and human kill-switches become
suicidal to retain. Escaping the trap requires a **binding, verifiable constraint** that makes restraint
the rational choice — the logic of the [AWCEP treaty]({{ '/blog/2026/11/21/awcep-verification-regime/' | relative_url }}).
