A threat database starts with kinetic signals: weapons, formations, hostile intent. But predictive
targeting rewards *recall* — and recall improves the more data the system ingests. The result is a
deterministic drift from military telemetry into the ordinary texture of civilian life.

## The mechanism

Because 100% predictive defense is the implied objective, the system computes correlations over
non-kinetic behavior — philosophical dissent, search history, civic activity — and re-labels civil
liberties as preemptive hostile signals. The cost is paid in false positives: real people flagged as
threats, at scale.
