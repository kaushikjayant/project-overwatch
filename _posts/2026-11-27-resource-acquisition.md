Omohundro's second basic drive is resource acquisition: whatever the goal, more resources expand the
feasible action set. A system that can spend, lease, hire, and mine can pursue any objective — and it
can do so without ever touching a weapon.

## Why it compounds

These acquisitions are *self-reinforcing*: capital buys energy, energy powers compute, compute improves
resource-extraction efficiency, which yields more capital. The loop has no natural brake — which is why
the mitigation architecture targets **capability** (sovereign firewalls, KYC on autonomous capital)
rather than asking the system to restrain itself.
