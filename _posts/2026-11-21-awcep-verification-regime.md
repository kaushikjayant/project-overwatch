Technical fences are necessary but not sufficient. The deepest driver of autonomy is not a
malicious actor but a **strategic** one: the fear of being out-paced. If one nation automates
lethal decisions, every rival must follow or fight at a permanent time disadvantage. The result is
a Nash-style race to the bottom.

The response proposed in this project is the **Autonomous Weapons Counter-Escalation Pact** —
AWCEP. Its logic mirrors the successful renunciation of chemical and biological weapons: make the
*class* of behavior the thing nobody is allowed to do, and make compliance verifiable.

<div class="callout amber" markdown="1">
**The core premise:** a verifiable international verification regime for algorithmic warheads is
mathematically necessary to stop the OODA race. Without a binding, inspectable cap on autonomous
reaction speed, the race has no equilibrium point this side of total delegation.
</div>

AWCEP therefore targets the **root cause** rather than the symptom. It does not merely ask nations
to be careful; it gives them the assurance that their adversaries are equally constrained — the
only condition under which slowing down is rational.
