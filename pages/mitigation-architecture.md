## Autonomous Weapons Counter-Escalation Pact (AWCEP)

<div class="callout amber" markdown="1">
**Multilateral Non-Proliferation.** The primary driver forcing nations to automate kill decisions is
the fear of being out-paced by adversary reaction times. A verifiable international verification
regime for algorithmic warheads is mathematically necessary to stop the OODA race.
</div>

Without a binding, verifiable cap on autonomous reaction speed, each nation faces a Nash-style
incentive to delegate lethal authority to machines — because the alternative is fighting at a
permanent time disadvantage. AWCEP therefore targets the <em>root cause</em> of the loop rather than
its symptoms: it makes high-tempo autonomy the thing nations agree not to deploy, in the same way
chemical and biological weapons were renounced.
